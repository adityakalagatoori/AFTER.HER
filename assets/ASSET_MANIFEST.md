# After Her — Visual Asset Manifest

Every real photograph used on this site is listed here with full provenance. If an image isn't listed here, it isn't a real photograph — it's an original CSS/SVG design element (paper texture, torn-edge dividers, redaction bars, etc.), not a reproduction of any real document, photograph, or person. See `research/asset-manifest.md` for the same records in structured JSON form, and `research/rejected-assets.md` for what was considered and declined.

Every image below was individually opened and visually inspected before use to confirm no identifiable person is a subject of the photograph — public building exteriors sometimes have incidental, unidentifiable bystanders in the distance (e.g. people playing cricket on a public ground, two people seated under a tree), and that was judged acceptable; a recognizable portrait would not have been.

## Images NOT included, and why

- **Newspaper front pages / clippings** — excluded entirely. Active publisher copyright; this project has no license to reproduce them, regardless of citation.
- **Survivor/victim portraits** — excluded entirely for every case, including Bhanwari Devi and Bilkis Bano, who are publicly self-identified activists. A citation to their public advocacy is included in their dossiers; a specific photograph of either woman was not sourced in this pass, since doing so responsibly requires a higher bar of review (consent context, not just license) that wasn't completed here.
- **Patiala House Courts, Delhi** — no verifiable Commons file found despite a dedicated search pass (see `research/rejected-assets.md`).
- **J&K-specific court building (as opposed to Punjab & Haryana HC, used instead)** — no verifiable Commons file found; the Punjab & Haryana HC image is used instead since it covers the actual trial venue's jurisdiction.

This manifest should be updated, not silently expanded, if more assets are added later — each new entry needs the same license/author/date verification, plus a visual inspection for identifiable people, before it's used.
